# Bebê realista: prompts de teste (semanas 4, 22 e 40)

Modelo pensado: Nano Banana Pro, 2K, 1:1. Mesmo bloco de luz e cor nos três para a evolução ficar contínua.

**Bloco comum (final de todo prompt):**
Lighting: soft warm amber and rose backlight through the womb, like light passing through the uterine wall, gentle shallow depth of field, floating particles in amniotic fluid. Color palette warm peach, amber, rose, deep red at the edges. Ultra detailed, scientific accuracy like Lennart Nilsson style embryology photography, no text, no labels, no watermark, no cartoon, no gore.

## Semana 4
Photorealistic medical-embryology macro photograph of a human embryo at 4 weeks of gestation (about 2 mm), inside the gestational sac, centered. Anatomically accurate: C-shaped curved embryo with a large head fold, visible neural tube along the back, paired somites like small beads along the spine, a bulging primitive heart tube visible through the translucent skin, pharyngeal arches at the neck, tail bud, attached yolk sac beside it connected by a short stalk, early chorionic villi on the sac wall. Skin fully translucent, thin blood vessels and the developing heart and liver bulge glowing faintly red inside.

## Semana 22
Photorealistic medical-embryology photograph of a human fetus at 22 weeks of gestation (about 28 cm, 430 g), floating inside the amniotic sac, centered, side profile, curled fetal position, one hand near the face. Anatomically accurate for 22 weeks: lean body with little fat, thin wrinkled translucent reddish skin with fine lanugo hair, eyelids still fused, defined lips, nose and ears, eyebrows starting, visible fingernails. Through the thin skin show realistic internal anatomy faintly: network of blood vessels across the head and torso, the beating heart region and the liver as darker shapes in the chest and abdomen, ribs subtly visible. Umbilical cord spiraling from the belly to the placenta with blue-red vessels.

## Semana 40
Photorealistic medical-embryology photograph of a full-term human fetus at 40 weeks of gestation (about 51 cm, 3.4 kg) inside the uterus, head-down cephalic position ready for birth, centered, tightly curled with knees to chest and arms crossed, filling most of the womb. Anatomically accurate for 40 weeks: chubby rounded body with baby fat, smooth opaque pink skin with traces of white vernix caseosa, almost no lanugo, open eyes or relaxed closed eyes, hair on the scalp, fully formed fingernails. Faint subsurface veins visible at the temples and eyelids. Umbilical cord thick and spiraling to the placenta on the uterine wall, uterine wall tissue visible around with fine capillaries.
